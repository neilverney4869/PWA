Authenticity, Craft & People First

Since stepping into the real estate industry in 1987, Peter has built a respected career spanning nearly four decades — as both a residential sales agent and part-time auctioneer. Known for his authenticity, professionalism and calm approach, he has earned the trust of buyers and sellers across changing markets and generations of families.

Peter's long-standing success is grounded in relationships and a sincere belief that people always come first. He is committed to achieving the best possible outcomes for his clients while leading every interaction with integrity, respect and care.

Passionate about continuous learning and refining the craft of auctioneering, Peter combines experience with genuine connection, strong communication and a collaborative style. He has a deep appreciation and understanding of the psychology behind an auction — applying this to performance, communication and decision-making.

Peter is known for his strengths-based approach, encouraging others to grow in confidence, refine their craft and perform at their best.

Philosophy & Values

The Walker Auctions Approach

Adaptability, collaboration and trust — the values at the heart of nearly four decades in real estate.

“Me.
We.”

The Shortest Poem. The Deepest Truth.

Muhammad Ali's iconic "Me, We" is often regarded as the shortest poem in the English language. Delivered spontaneously on June 4, 1975, at Harvard University, Ali responded with two simple yet powerful words: "Me. We." That sentiment perfectly reflects Peter's core values.

01

Adaptability

After 39 years in the real estate industry, Peter has learned that adaptability allows you to navigate change with confidence — reading shifting markets, evolving buyer behaviour, and the unique dynamics of every auction with clarity and calm.

02

Collaboration

Collaboration creates the best outcomes for everyone involved. Peter works closely with agents, vendors and buyers — not as an authority, but as a partner — to ensure every voice is heard and every campaign is built on shared understanding.

03

Trust

Trust builds strong relationships, and strong relationships build lasting success. It is the foundation of everything Peter does — earned through consistency, honesty, and a genuine belief that people always come first.

“It's not about me. It's about we.”
