Experience Meets Authenticity

After 39 years in the industry, Peter Walker has built a reputation for professionalism, consistency, and results. He is known for his genuine approach, strong relationships, and ability to personalise every auction experience.

Peter combines deep product knowledge with a high level of empathy, allowing him to connect with clients while maintaining control in high-pressure environments — whether working with first-time sellers or seasoned investors.

Peter brings a calm, composed and highly professional presence to every auction. Backed by extensive compliance knowledge and sharp communication skills, he connects effortlessly with buyers and sellers, creating confidence, competition and exceptional results.

Outside of real estate, Peter enjoys time with his family, following cricket throughout the summer, and supporting his sons' sporting pursuits.
